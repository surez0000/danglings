01 · Charms

Thirty little faces, or any emoji you like.

Prefer something quieter than a creature? Charm mode hangs one small emoji from a physically simulated thread: seven joints, real gravity, a breath of wind. Flick it and it swings back; click it and it performs its ritual with a sound synthesized on the spot.

Any emoji works too: type it into the picker and it hangs on the same thread.

02 · Companions

Or a creature on a swing that watches you work.

Twelve rigged 3D companions sit on a swing hung from the top edge. Their heads follow your cursor, they chirp when you say hi, wag and wiggle when idle, and doze off when you settle into work. Several come in colour variants.

Rendered on demand with three.js: when nothing moves, nothing renders. A sleeping companion costs 0 GPU frames, 0 timers, and no messages between the window and the app. It wakes on your cursor, not on a clock.

03 · Reminders

Quietly useful. It counts desk time, not wall-clock time.

Three gentle clocks and as many of your own as you want, delivered by the character itself as a small bubble with a soft chime. No system notifications, nothing to dismiss in a hurry. Shown here at the app's real proportions.

  • 💧
    Water, every 45 minutes

    “Drank one” logs a glass toward a daily eight. Only desk minutes count.

  • 🚶
    Move, after 50 minutes

    Continuous desk time, from your last keypress or mouse move. Five minutes away restarts it, because you already moved.

  • 👀
    Eye rest, 20-20-20

    Every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Off by default, one toggle to turn on.

  • ⏰
    Your own

    Meds at 09:00 and 21:00, the stand-up at 09:25, eye drops every 90 minutes. Any interval, or fixed times on chosen weekdays.

  • 🌙
    Away means away

    Idle five minutes and every clock pauses. Nothing fires in your first minute back at the desk.

  • 👆
    One click, or none

    Click the bubble to acknowledge, right-click to snooze ten minutes. Preview any reminder from the menu before you commit to it.

04 · Built to disappear

An overlay that stays out of the way, by design.

The whole window is transparent and passes clicks through to whatever is behind it. Only the thread and the character catch the mouse. Nothing else on your screen knows it's there.

Window
Transparent, always on top, click-through everywhere except within 42 px of the thread. Follows you across Spaces and over fullscreen apps on macOS.
Idle cost
Zero rendered frames, zero timers ticking the loop. The desk-time clock asks the OS once every 5 s — one number, no polling of your input.
Permissions
None. No accessibility access, no screen recording, no notifications. Idle time comes from a public OS call.
Sound
Every chirp, chime and ritual is synthesized in the app with a generated reverb. No audio files, no volume spikes.
Updates
Signed manifests on GitHub Releases, checked a few times a day. The companion holds up a small gift; one click installs and restarts.
Made of
Tauri 2 (Rust) · React 19 · three.js · MIT licensed · source on GitHub

Version 1.0.0 · all releases & notes

macOS · first open

Not notarized yet (no paid developer account), so once: right-click Danglings → Open. If macOS calls it damaged, run xattr -cr /Applications/Danglings.app in Terminal. After that it opens like anything else, and updates install from inside the app.

Windows · first open

SmartScreen may say “Windows protected your PC” because the installer isn't code-signed. Click More info → Run anyway.

Find it again

Menu bar / tray icon, or Shift+Alt+K. Right-click the charm for companions, reminders and settings.
